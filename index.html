Our Story

Two brothers starting small, with a dream to build big.

AVAB Commercial Cleaning began with a simple idea shared by two brothers, Abhishek Lamichhane and Avinash Yogi: work hard, treat people fairly, and build something meaningful.

We are starting small, but our dream is big. We want to earn trust one workplace at a time, create opportunities for people, support our families and contribute positively to our community.

For us, cleaning is more than making a space look good. It is about helping people feel comfortable, safe and proud of the places where they work.

Every client matters. Every commitment matters. We believe that honesty, consistency and attention to detail can turn a small beginning into a business people are proud to recommend.

One team. One dream. A cleaner tomorrow.
